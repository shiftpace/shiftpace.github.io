Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026. This policy explains what ShiftPace Planners LLC (“ShiftPace”, “we”, “us”) collects on shiftpace.github.io and through our forms and checkout, why, and the choices you have.

Short version: we collect your name and email when you fill out one of our forms, Stripe handles payments (we never see your card number), and we use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used. We don’t sell your personal information. Email shiftpacellc@gmail.com to unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data.

Who we are

ShiftPace Planners LLC is a limited liability company registered in the United States. We sell digital Google Sheets planners. Our team operates the business remotely, and some of the people who run it are located outside the United States (including in Uzbekistan), so your information may be accessed from outside the U.S. Contact: shiftpacellc@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us through Google Forms

When you request the free planner, apply to be a tester or leave a review, you fill out a Google Form. Depending on the form, this includes your name, email address and your answers (for example, what you’d use a planner for, or your review). Google Forms is operated by Google LLC.

Purchases through Stripe

Checkout happens on Stripe’s secure payment pages. Stripe collects and processes your payment details. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us what we need to fulfill and support your order, such as your name, email, the product purchased, the amount, the date and limited payment details (for example, card brand and last four digits). See Stripe’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics: Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4, which uses cookies and similar technology to collect information such as pages viewed, buttons clicked (for example, “Buy” or “Get it free”), how far you scroll, how you arrived at the site (for example, from Pinterest or YouTube), approximate location (city/country, derived from your IP address), device, browser and language. We use this in aggregate to see what works on the site. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Analytics: Microsoft Clarity, including session recordings

We use Microsoft Clarity, which uses cookies and similar technology to create heatmaps and recordings of how visitors move, click and scroll on our pages. These recordings help us find confusing or broken parts of the site. Clarity is set by Microsoft to mask text typed into fields by default, and our site has no fields where you enter payment details. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use it

  • To deliver the products and free planner you request, and to answer support questions.
  • To select and communicate with testers, and to display reviews you choose to submit (with the name you provide, or as you request).
  • To occasionally email you about ShiftPace planners if you gave us your email. Every such email lets you unsubscribe.
  • To understand and improve the site (which pages and products people look at, what isn’t working).
  • To keep records required for taxes and accounting, and to prevent fraud.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t exchange it for money or anything of value.
  • We don’t use your information for targeted advertising on other websites (cross-context behavioral advertising).
  • We don’t see or store your full payment card number.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run parts of our business for us: Google (Forms, Sheets, Gmail, Analytics), Stripe (payments), Microsoft (Clarity) and GitHub (website hosting, which may log technical data such as IP addresses). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Cookies and your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on.
  • Do Not Track: our site does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals, because there is no common standard for them.

Unsubscribe, access or delete your data

Email shiftpacellc@gmail.com from the address you used with us and tell us what you’d like: to stop receiving emails, to get a copy of the information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We’ll reply and act on your request within a reasonable time. We may need to keep certain purchase records where the law requires it (for example, for tax purposes), and we can’t delete data held by Stripe, Google or Microsoft under their own policies, but we will delete what we control and tell you what we couldn’t delete.

How long we keep it

We keep form responses and email contacts for as long as they’re useful for the purpose you gave them to us, or until you ask us to delete them. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for accounting and tax obligations. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity.

Security

We use reputable providers and protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-step verification. No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our site and products are meant for adults. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

California residents (CCPA & CalOPPA)

In the past 12 months we have collected these categories of personal information: identifiers (name, email address, IP address), commercial information (products purchased), internet or network activity (pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, session recordings) and approximate geolocation. The sources, purposes and recipients are described above. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not knowingly sell or share information of consumers under 16.

ShiftPace is a small business and may not meet the thresholds that make the California Consumer Privacy Act apply to it. Regardless, if you live in California you can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, to correct it or to delete it, by emailing shiftpacellc@gmail.com. We won’t treat you differently for making a request. We may need to confirm your request comes from you, for example by asking you to write from the email address you used with us.

Under CalOPPA: you can review and request changes to your information as described above; our approach to “Do Not Track” is explained in the Cookies section; and third parties (Google and Microsoft) may collect information about your online activities on our site through their analytics tools.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll add a notice on the site.
