Questions

Do I need a big following?

No. You don’t need to post anything on your own accounts. Just send the video to us.

What if I don’t like the planners?

Then say so in your video. That feedback helps us improve, and it’s exactly what we’re asking for.

Do I have to show my real finances?

No. Use example numbers or blur anything personal. We care about how the planner works for you, not your actual balances.

What if life gets busy and I can’t make a video?

Just let us know by email. We’d rather hear that than nothing.

Can I post my review elsewhere?

On your own social accounts, sure — please keep the disclosure line in it. Please don’t post it as a review on review sites like Google or Trustpilot, since they don’t allow reviews for free products.
