FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this a physical product?

No. These are digital Google Sheets templates. Nothing will be shipped.

Do I need Excel or any paid software?

No. You only need a free Google account. The planners are built for Google Sheets and run in your browser.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and the copy in your Google Drive is yours to keep and use.

Do you see my data?

No. When you make a copy, it lives in your own Google Drive. We can’t see or edit your copy.

Do I have to connect my bank account?

No. You enter your own numbers. Nothing is linked to your bank.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, with the Google Sheets app. For first-time setup and the best view of the dashboards, we recommend a computer.

What’s the difference between Money Reset and Paycheck Budget Planner?

Both use the same paycheck-based monthly budget. The 2027 Money Reset adds a full debt payoff plan (snowball, avalanche or custom) with your debt-free date. If you don’t have debt to pay off, the Paycheck Budget Planner is all you need. If you buy the Money Reset, you don’t need the Paycheck planner too.

How do I get started after buying?

Follow the Getting Started guide. Every planner also has a START HERE tab inside.

I lost my link. What do I do?

Email us at shiftpacellc@gmail.com with the email you used at checkout, and we’ll send it again.

What is your refund policy?

Because you get instant access to a digital file, sales are generally final. But if a planner doesn’t work as described or you have a problem with your link, email us within 14 days of purchase and we’ll fix it or refund you.

Is this financial advice?

No. ShiftPace planners are organizing tools. They don’t provide financial, tax or legal advice.

Who is behind ShiftPace?

ShiftPace is a small independent brand run by ShiftPace Planners LLC. We design simple, good-looking planners we’d want to use ourselves.
