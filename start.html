Getting started

Thanks for your purchase! Here’s how to set up your planner in about 5 minutes.

Set up any planner

1

Open your PDF and click the planner link

Use a computer for the first setup. Sign in to your Google account if asked (a free account works).

2

Click “Make a copy”

Google saves your own private copy to your Google Drive. Rename it if you like. Only you can see it.

3

Read the START HERE tab

Every planner has a START HERE tab with a short walkthrough of that planner.

4

Fill in SETUP once

Enter your starting info in the highlighted cells on the SETUP tab. Every other tab reads from it.

5

Clear the sample data and start logging

Planners come filled with sample numbers so you can see how they work. Delete them from the input cells, then add your own. The dashboard updates by itself.

Golden rule: type only in the highlighted input cells. Don’t edit cells with formulas, the DASHBOARD, or any tab whose name starts with CALC_ — those do the math for you. Made a mistake? Use File → Version history to go back.

Quick start by planner

2027 Money Reset
  1. SETUP: enter your paychecks, budget categories and savings goals once a year.
  2. JAN–DEC: plan each paycheck, then log what you spend in the transactions list.
  3. DEBT PAYOFF: list your debts to see your debt-free date.
  4. DASHBOARD & YEAR: just look — they update on their own.
Debt Payoff Tracker
  1. SETUP: choose your strategy (snowball, avalanche or custom) and your extra monthly payment.
  2. Add up to 25 debts with balance, interest rate (APR) and minimum payment.
  3. DEBT PAYOFF: see your debt-free date, interest saved and the order to attack each debt.
Paycheck Budget Planner
  1. SETUP: enter your paychecks and budget categories.
  2. JAN–DEC: assign every paycheck before you spend it, then log your transactions.
  3. DASHBOARD & YEAR: track your progress rings and spending charts.
Small Business & Etsy Seller Tracker
  1. SETUP: add your products with list price and product cost.
  2. SALES: log each order — date, platform, product, quantity. Leave price blank to use the list price. Fees, cost and net profit calculate automatically.
  3. EXPENSES: log business costs and tick the tax-deductible box where it applies.
  4. DASHBOARD: see revenue, take-home profit, tax set-aside, best month, top platform and top products — it updates by itself.
2027 Habit & Goals Tracker
  1. SETUP: add your habits and your word of the year.
  2. GOALS: set your yearly goals and milestones.
  3. JAN–DEC: tick a box each day you do a habit. Streaks and scores update automatically.
  4. DASHBOARD: watch your year-at-a-glance heatmap fill up.

Using your phone

Install the free Google Sheets app and open your copy from Google Drive. Phones are great for quick logging; use a computer for setup and to see the full dashboard.
